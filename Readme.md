# Video Shelf

**Your private video collection, beautifully catalogued.**

Video Shelf is a Windows desktop app for cataloguing video collections spread across many hard drives. It keeps a searchable catalogue of every folder and scene, with screenshots, ratings and play history, so you can browse everything even when a drive isn't connected.

## Download

Get the installer from the [latest release](https://github.com/dee4life2005/Media-Library-Installers/releases/latest). Once it's installed, the app tells you when a new version is available.

The installer isn't signed yet, so Windows may warn you about it. On a PC with Smart App Control switched on, it may be blocked.

## Requirements

- Windows 10 version 1809 or later, or Windows 11 (64-bit)
- ffmpeg, a free separate download. The app shows you how to get it.

## Highlights

- **Browse visually:** folder and scene tiles in Landscape, Square or Portrait, with a hover preview that scrubs through each video.
- **Find anything:** search, range filters, tags, your own categories and saved filters.
- **Stay in control:** rate and play from anywhere, or select many scenes and act on them together.
- **Explore:** a Performers window and a filterable Dashboard.
- **Keep it tidy:** Collection Health, Duplicate Scenes and Clean Up show what needs attention.
- **Keep it safe:** safety copies on another drive, a full data mirror and an optional PIN.
- **Make it yours:** Normal and Dark appearance, adjustable tiles and a demo mode for showing the app to others.

## Documentation

The [User Guide (PDF)](User-Guide.pdf) covers every feature. It's also on the app's Help menu.

## Your data

Everything stays on your PC. The catalogue, screenshots and settings are kept in a local data folder. The app goes online to check for updates, which you can switch off in **Settings > Privacy**.

Uninstalling removes the app and leaves your data in place.
